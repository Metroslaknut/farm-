# Financial test cases

Money moves only through a double-entry ledger (`wallet_transactions`, append-only), driven by payment/refund/payout state machines and
HMAC-signed provider callbacks. Every case below is an executable test; "invariants" means the checks listed at the end hold afterwards.
Mock provider: no real payment adapter ships yet (`PAYMENT_PROVIDER=none` in production), so these tests prove OUR side of the contract — see risks.

## System-wide invariant test (`qa/financial-qa`)

Twelve complete sales run **in parallel** through the real HTTP API and callbacks: payment with 8 duplicate callbacks each, then a quarter stay paid, a quarter are
delivered, a quarter are cancelled and refunded (three finance approvals racing, three duplicate refund callbacks), a quarter are delivered and withdrawn (four identical
payout requests, two approvals, three duplicate paid callbacks). Afterwards:

- every order was paid exactly once; every captured payment has exactly 3 ledger lines (provider, seller, shipping), never more;
- exactly 3 refunds and 3 payouts reached the provider (call counters), 3 refunds succeeded, 3 withdrawals paid;
- **the ledger sums to zero**, every wallet's balances equal the sum of its own ledger lines, no seller bucket is negative, no ledger line exists twice;
- platform commission equals the fee of the sales that still stand (refunded sales returned theirs), computed independently from the orders with the commission function;
- sellers hold exactly what they earned minus what was paid out;
- the platform's own **reconciliation** (`POST /admin/finance/reconcile`) reports no unbalanced journal, negative balance, wallet drift or missing journal, and the **financial audit chain verifies**.

Other integrity tests already in `finance`: balance-drift detection and repair, crash recovery (succeeded-but-unapplied payments, missing journals), provider-vs-ledger comparison, hash-chained
audit log with tamper detection, payout account encryption and masking, wallets and ledgers visible only to their owner/finance staff.

## Invariants every financial test checks

1. Σ ledger lines = 0 (double entry). 2. Wallet balance = Σ its ledger lines per bucket. 3. At most one applied payment per order; at most one refund per payment; one provider request per refund/payout.
2. Money held by a request cannot be spent twice. 5. Idempotency-Key replays return the original result. 6. Provider callbacks are authenticated, replay-safe and amount-checked.
3. Staff cannot approve their own money movements. 8. Nothing sensitive (account numbers, addresses) appears in the audit log or responses.
