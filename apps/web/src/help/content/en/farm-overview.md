When you open "My farms", the farm's "Overview" opens.

## The overview

- **Key figures**: animals on the farm, sales and weigh-ins
- **Need a growth check**: animals not weighed for a while or outside the standard. Press "View all" to record.
- **Animals by type**: how many animals of each type you have

## The farm menu

- **Animals**: the animal list, adding animals and lineage
- **Farm records**: health, weights, feeding, expenses and breeding. See [farm records](help:farm-records).
- **Trade**: listings and trades. See [list an animal for sale](help:list-animal-for-sale).
- **Analytics**: charts and figures over time

What you see depends on your role on the farm. On a phone, press the menu button at the top left to open the menu.

## Search and notifications

- Press the search icon at the top and type an animal code or name.
- The bell shows new notifications such as offers, orders and invitations.
