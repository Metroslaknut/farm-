A farm holds all your animal records. If you cannot create one yet, [request farm-owner access](help:request-farm-owner) first.

## Steps

1. Open "My farms". If you have the access but no farm yet, you are taken to "Set up your farm" automatically.
   ![Set up your farm, step 1 farm profile](img:1)
2. Fill in "Farm name", e.g. [Example] Sukjai Farm.
   ![The farm name filled in](img:2)
3. Under "Animal types raised", tick every kind of animal you raise. You can pick several.
   ![The animal type check boxes](img:3)
4. Press "Next".
   ![The Next button](img:4)
5. Choose the province, the district and the sub-district (optional).
   ![Province, district and sub-district fields](img:5)
6. Click the map where the farm is to place the pin, then press "Next".
   ![The map for placing the farm pin](img:6)
7. Add a phone, email or LINE ID (optional, but it helps buyers reach you), then press "Save and start".
   ![Contact details and the Save and start button](img:7)
8. The farm's "Overview" opens. You can start adding animals.
   ![The overview of the new farm](img:8)

:::note You can change it later
The name, animal types, location and contact details can be edited under "Farm details" in the farm menu.
:::

:::trouble "Save and start" says you are not allowed
Your account does not have farm-owner access yet. See [request farm-owner access](help:request-farm-owner).
:::
