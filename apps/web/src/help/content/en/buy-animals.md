## Find animals and make an offer

1. Open the "Livestock market". Type a type or breed in the search box, and filter by price, animal type and province.
   ![The market search and filters](img:1)
2. Open a listing to see the price and details. Press "☆ Save" to keep it for later.
   ![A listing with its price and the Save button](img:2)
3. Under "Make an offer", enter your price and press "Send offer". For a fixed price, press "Request to buy".
   ![The Make an offer section](img:3)
4. The negotiation opens: wait for the seller to accept, decline or counter. All your offers are under "My offers".
   ![The negotiation waiting for the seller](img:4)

## Check out and receive the animal

5. Once a price is agreed, press "Proceed to checkout", choose collection or delivery, then press "Place order".
6. Pay and follow the status under "My orders". When it arrives, press "I received the animal".
7. Refunds and payment history are in the "Wallet".

:::note Ask the seller first
Press "Message seller" on the listing. Never pay outside FARM+.
:::

:::trouble Checkout says the reservation has ended
An agreed price is held for a limited time. Go back to the negotiation and agree with the seller again.
:::
