Use the email or phone number you registered with, together with your password. If you registered with Google, press "Continue with Google" instead.

## Steps

1. Press "Sign in" at the top right of the page (on a phone, press the person icon at the top right)
   ![The Sign in button at the top right](img:1)
2. Type your email or mobile number in "Email or phone"
   ![Email or phone filled in](img:2)
3. Type your "Password"
   ![Password filled in](img:3)
4. Press "Sign in"
   ![The Sign in button below the form](img:4)
5. If two-step verification is on, open the authenticator app on your phone and type the code in "Authenticator or recovery code"
   ![The Authenticator or recovery code field](img:5)
6. Press "Verify". You are signed in
   ![The Verify button below the code field](img:6)

:::trouble It says "Verify your email or phone number before signing in."
You have not opened the verification link in the email, or not entered the SMS code yet.

- Press "Send the link again" below the message
- Open the email from FARM+, press the verification link, then come back and sign in
  :::

:::trouble It says "The sign-in details or password are incorrect."

- Check the spelling of your email or number, and that your keyboard is not set to Thai
- If you forgot your password, press "Forgot your password?" and follow [Forgot your password](help:forgot-password)
  :::

:::trouble It says "Too many attempts. Please wait a moment and try again."
There were too many failed sign-ins. Wait about 15 minutes, then try again
:::

:::trouble A link opened in a new tab asks you to sign in again
Sometimes a new tab does not know you are signed in yet. Sign in again in that tab, or go back to the original tab
:::
