Registering is free and needs only one email address. After registering you must verify your email before you can sign in.

## Steps

1. Press "Register" at the top right of the page (on a phone, press the person icon at the top right first, then "Register")
   ![The Register button at the top right](img:1)
2. Under "Register with", choose "Email"
   ![The Email tab on the registration page](img:2)
3. Fill in "Display name", "Email" and a "Password" of at least 10 characters, then type it again in "Confirm password"
   ![Name, email and password filled in](img:3)
4. Press "Register"
   ![The Register button below the form](img:4)
5. You will see "Check your email". Open the email from FARM+ and press the verification link
   ![The Check your email page](img:5)
6. When you see "Your email is verified", press "Sign in"
   ![The Your email is verified page](img:6)

:::trouble The email did not arrive

- Look in your spam or promotions folder
- On the "Check your email" page, press "Send the link again"
- If you already closed that page, go to [Sign in](/farms/sign-in), enter your email and password, then press "Send the link again"
  :::

:::trouble The link has expired or does not work
The page says "The verification link has expired or is invalid". Enter your email and press "Send a new verification link"
:::

:::trouble It says "This email already has an account"
You registered with this email before. [Sign in](help:sign-in) instead. If you forgot the password, see [Forgot your password](help:forgot-password)
:::
