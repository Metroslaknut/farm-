If you already have a Google (Gmail) account, you can use it to join FARM+. You do not have to wait for a verification email.

## Steps

1. Press "Register" at the top right of the page (on a phone, press the person icon at the top right first, then "Register")
   ![The Register button at the top right](img:1)
2. Press "Continue with Google"
   ![The Continue with Google button on the registration page](img:2)
3. On the Google page, choose the account to use, or enter your Google email and password
4. Wait a moment. You are brought back to FARM+ and signed in
   ![FARM+ after signing in with Google](img:4)

:::note Facebook works too
Press "Continue with Facebook" instead and follow the same steps
:::

:::trouble It says "This email already has an account. Sign in with your password, then connect it later."
You registered with the same email before and set a password. [Sign in](help:sign-in) with your email and password first. If you forgot the password, see [Forgot your password](help:forgot-password)
:::

:::trouble It says "Sign-in was cancelled"
You pressed cancel or closed the Google page before it finished. Press "Back to sign in" and try "Continue with Google" again
:::
