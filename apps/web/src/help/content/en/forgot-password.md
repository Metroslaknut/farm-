If you cannot remember your password, ask for a reset link. We send it to the email or phone number you registered with.

## Steps

1. On the sign-in page, press "Forgot your password?"
   ![The Forgot your password link on the sign-in page](img:1)
2. Type the email or mobile number you registered with in "Email or phone"
   ![The forgot-password page with the Email or phone field](img:2)
3. Press "Send a reset link"
   ![The Send a reset link button](img:3)
4. When you see "If this account exists, we have sent it a reset link", open the email or SMS from FARM+ and press the link
   ![The message saying the reset link was sent](img:4)
5. On the "Set a new password" page, enter a "New password" of at least 10 characters
   ![The Set a new password page with the New password field](img:5)
6. Type it again in "Confirm new password"
   ![Confirm new password filled in](img:6)
7. Press "Save the new password"
   ![The Save the new password button](img:7)
8. When you see "Your new password is saved", press "Sign in" and use the new password
   ![The Your new password is saved message and the Sign in button](img:8)

:::note Other devices must sign in again
After you set a new password, other phones or computers that were signed in have to sign in again with the new password
:::

:::trouble The email or SMS did not arrive

- Look in your spam or promotions folder
- Check that the email or number matches the one you registered with. For safety, we never say whether an account exists
- Wait a moment, then ask for a new link at [Forgot your password](/farms/forgot-password)
- If you registered with Google, sign in with "Continue with Google" instead
  :::

:::trouble It says "The reset link has expired or is invalid"
A link works only once and for a limited time. Press "Ask for a new link", repeat step 2, and use only the latest link
:::
