## Post in the community

1. Open "Community" and type in "Share with the community".
   ![The Share with the community box](img:1)
2. Press "Add photo or video" to attach a file.
3. Choose "Who can see this", then press "Post".
   ![Who can see this and the Post button](img:2)

## Upload a video

1. Open "Videos" and go to "Upload a video".
2. Choose the "Video file", enter a "Title" and choose "Who can watch".
   ![The video upload form](img:3)
3. Press "Upload" and follow the status under "My videos". The video shows once processing is done.

## Go live

Only approved farm owners and sellers can go live.

1. Open "Live" and go to the page for starting a stream.
2. Enter a "Title", choose "Who can watch", then press "Create live stream".
   ![The create live stream form](img:4)
3. Press "Show stream settings", put the address and key into a streaming app such as OBS, and start broadcasting.
4. When you finish, press "End stream".

:::trouble It says video uploads or going live are not available yet
The service is not switched on yet. You can still watch existing videos and streams.
:::

:::trouble "Create live stream" says you are not allowed
[Request farm-owner access](help:request-farm-owner) or [become a seller](help:verify-identity-seller) first.
:::
