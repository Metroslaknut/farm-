Each animal has its own page for weights, health, breeding and listing it for sale. You need a [farm](help:create-farm) first.

## Steps

1. In your farm, open "Animals" and press "Register animal".
   ![The Register animal button on the animal list](img:1)
2. "Animal code" can be left blank: one is made for you, e.g. A-00001.
   ![The animal code field](img:2)
3. Choose the "Animal type" first, then the "Breed".
   ![The animal type and breed fields](img:3)
4. Choose the "Sex" and enter the "Weight (kg)" if you have weighed it (optional).
   ![The sex and weight fields](img:4)
5. Enter the "Birth date". If you don't know it, enter an approximate age and press "Use approximate age".
   ![The birth date and approximate age fields](img:5)
6. If you know the parents, choose the "Sire (father)" and "Dam (mother)" from your animals.
   ![The parents section](img:6)
7. Press "Register animal".
   ![The Register animal button](img:7)
8. The animal's page opens. From there go to "Growth", "Health", "Pedigree" or "List for sale".
   ![The page of the newly registered animal](img:8)

:::note Animal codes
A code must be unique on the farm and cannot be changed later.
:::

:::trouble No "Register animal" button
Your role on this farm may be view-only. Ask the farm owner to change your access. See [farm staff](help:farm-staff).
:::

:::trouble The breed cannot be chosen
Choose the animal type first. If the type you need is missing, add it to the farm's "Animal types raised".
:::
