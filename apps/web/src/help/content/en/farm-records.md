There are five kinds of farm records: health, weights, feeding, expenses and breeding. They all work the same way.

## Add a record

1. In the farm menu, open "Farm records" and choose a kind, e.g. "Weights".
   ![The weight records list](img:1)
2. Press the "Add …" button, e.g. "Add weight".
   ![The Add weight button](img:2)
3. Choose the animal, enter the date and details, then press "Save weight".
   ![A filled-in weight form](img:3)

## Find records

4. Filter by animal, kind and the "From" and "To" dates. Press "Reset" to see everything.
   ![The filters of the record list](img:4)

## Correct or void a wrong record

5. Open the record. Press "Correct" to change a value (you give a reason), or "Void" if the whole entry was a mistake.
   ![A record page with the Correct and Void buttons](img:5)
6. A voided record is not deleted. Tick "Show voided" to see it.
   ![The Show voided check box](img:6)

:::note Why records cannot be deleted
Every change is kept in the "Change history" with who made it and why, so your farm data can be trusted when you trade.
:::

:::trouble No add, correct or void buttons
What you can do depends on your role on the farm; for example workers can add records but not void them. Ask the farm owner. See [farm staff](help:farm-staff).
:::
