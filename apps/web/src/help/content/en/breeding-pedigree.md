## Record a breeding

1. In the farm menu, open "Farm records" and choose "Breeding".
2. Press "Add breeding record".
3. Choose the dam, then "Sire on this farm" or "Unknown / outside sire" (you can enter a name or a semen straw code).
4. Enter the "Breeding date". The expected delivery date is worked out for you. Then save.

## When you know the outcome

1. Open the breeding record and press "Record what happened".
2. Choose the status: pregnant, delivered or failed.
3. After a birth, press "Add offspring". The offspring's breed is set from the parents; you can change it for each one.
4. For poultry, use "Start incubation" and "Record hatching" instead of a delivery.

## Pedigrees

- On an animal's page, press "Pedigree" to see its parents, grandparents and offspring.
- The "Breeding & lineage" menu shows the breeding and birth records of the whole farm.

:::note Pedigrees come from what you recorded
If the sire or dam was not entered when you [registered the animal](help:add-animal), the pedigree shows them as unknown.
:::

:::trouble No button to add a breeding record
You need breeding access on this farm. See [farm staff](help:farm-staff).
:::
