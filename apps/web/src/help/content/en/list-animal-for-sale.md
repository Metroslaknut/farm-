You need to be an [approved seller](help:verify-identity-seller) and the owner or manager of a farm.

## Steps

1. Open the "Livestock market", press "Sell", then "Open listings" on your farm. Or choose "Sell animals" in the farm menu.
   ![The Open listings button on the Sell animals page](img:1)
2. Press "New listing".
   ![The New listing button](img:2)
3. Under "The animal", choose from your animals. Type, breed, sex and age are copied in for you.
   ![The animal to sell](img:3)
4. Enter the "Asking price (฿)" and the "Lowest offer you would consider", which buyers do not see. Tick "Open to offers" to allow negotiation.
   ![The price fields and Open to offers](img:4)
5. Add photo links, then press "Save as draft".
   ![The photos and the Save as draft button](img:5)
6. On the listing page, press "Submit for review". A moderator checks it before it appears in the market.
   ![The Submit for review button on the listing page](img:6)

## When buyers are interested

- Offers and orders are under "Trades" in the farm menu. You can accept, decline or counter.
- Money from sales is under "My earnings and withdrawals".

:::note What buyers see
Buyers see the copied animal details and the photos. Health and breeding records stay private to your farm.
:::

:::trouble Saving is blocked: only approved sellers can list
Press "Apply to become a seller" in that message. See [verify your identity and become a seller](help:verify-identity-seller).
:::

:::trouble A moderator rejected the listing
Read the reason on the listing page, press "Edit", fix it, then press "Submit for review" again.
:::
