This article shows the whole path for a new user. Press the article name in each step to read the detailed how-to.

## Steps

1. Everyone starts by registering, verifying and signing in. Then choose your path: raise animals, sell, or buy
   ![Diagram: register, verify, sign in, then three paths: request farm-owner access, create a farm, add animals; or verify identity, apply as a seller, list for sale; or buy right away](img:1)
2. Register with your [email](help:register-email), your [phone number](help:register-phone) or your [Google account](help:register-google)
3. Verify your account: press the link in the email, or enter the 6-digit code from the SMS
4. [Sign in](help:sign-in). If you cannot remember your password, see [Forgot your password](help:forgot-password)
5. To raise and record animals: [request farm-owner access](help:request-farm-owner), then create a farm and add your animals
6. To sell animals or products: [verify your identity and become a seller](help:verify-identity-seller), then list for sale
7. To buy: you can buy right away in the [Shop](/shop) or the [Livestock market](/market)

:::note Extra security
We recommend turning on [two-step verification](help:two-step-verification) so nobody else can get into your account
:::
