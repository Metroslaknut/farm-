Only approved sellers can list animals for sale and sell products in the shop. You send two things: your identity details and a seller application. The platform team reviews them.

## Steps

1. Open the "My account" menu at the top right
   ![The My account menu at the top right](img:1)
2. Press "Become a seller"
   ![Become a seller in the account menu](img:2)
3. Scroll down to "Identity verification" and write details in "Details to help verify your identity (optional)", e.g. your name as on your ID card
   ![The identity verification section with the details field](img:3)
4. In "Document 1 type", type ID card copy, then paste the file link in "Document 1 link (https)"
   ![Document type and document link filled in](img:4)
5. Press "Send identity details"
   ![The Send identity details button](img:5)
6. Go up to "Seller application" and fill in "Shop or business name (optional)", e.g. [Example] Sukjai Farm
   ![The seller application section with the shop name filled in](img:6)
7. Write what you will sell in "Anything the reviewers should know (optional)". For more documents, press "Add a document"
   ![The note to the reviewers field and the Add a document button](img:7)
8. Press "Send seller application"
   ![The Send seller application button](img:8)
9. Come back to this page to see the status badge of your application
   ![The status badge and the date the application was sent](img:9)

## How to make a document link with Google Drive

The document fields take a link that starts with https. They do not upload files. You can make a link in the Google Drive app on your phone.

- Step 1: Take a photo or scan of your ID card copy and upload it to Google Drive
- Step 2: Press the three dots on the file and choose "Share" or "Manage access"
- Step 3: Under "General access", change it to "Anyone with the link"
- Step 4: Press "Copy link", then come back and paste it in "Document 1 link (https)"

:::warning Protect your personal data
Anyone with the link can open the file. Do not send the link to other people. Write "For FARM+ seller application only" across the copy, and turn sharing off once you are approved
:::

## What each status means

- **Pending**: sent and waiting for the team to start. Nothing to do
- **Under review**: the team is looking at your application. Wait for the result
- **Approved**: you can start selling. Press "List an animal for sale" or "Sell products in the shop"
- **Rejected**: not approved. Read the reason, fix it and apply again
- **Suspended**: you cannot apply again. Contact the platform team

:::trouble Your application was "Rejected"

- Read "Reason from the reviewer" below the status badge
- Fix what it says, e.g. make the link open for anyone or add a document
- Press "Apply again"
  :::

:::trouble The reviewer says they cannot open your document
The link is not shared as "Anyone with the link". Repeat step 3 of the Google Drive guide, then send again
:::
